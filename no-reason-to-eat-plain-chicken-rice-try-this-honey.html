Ingredients (Makes 8 Servings)

Honey Garlic Chicken
- 1300g Raw Boneless Chicken Thighs, cubed and fat trimmed
- 2.5 Tsp Black Pepper
- 4 Tsp Garlic Granules
- 3 Tsp Onion Powder
- 2 Tsp Chilli Flakes
- 6 Tsp Olive Oil
- 50g Honey
- 30g Light Soy Sauce 
- 25g Dark Soy Sauce

Creamy Sriracha Sauce
- 300g Low Fat Yogurt 
- 120g Light Mayo
- 50g Sriracha
- 25g Honey
- 1.5 Tsp Smoked Paprika

Toasted Sesame Fried Rice
- 450-500g Frozen Mixed Vegetables (i had carrots, sweetcorn, green beans, peas)
- 100g Green Onion, white part
- 40g Sesame Seeds
- 350-400g Uncooked White Rice (700-800g Cooked)
- 40g Light Soy Sauce (adjust based on colour)
- 50g Green Onion, green part
- 20g Light Butter, gives silky smooth texture to rice
