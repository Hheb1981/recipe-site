Easiest Most Delicious High Protein Korean Beef Rice Bowl...

484
Calories
50g

Ingredients

Ingredients (for 4 portions)

- 800g Lean Beef Mince (I use topside beef one of the leanest cuts)
- 80g Low Sodium Soy Sauce (You can mix light and dark soy sauce)
- 30g Honey
- 4-5 Garlic Cloves Minced
- 25g Gochujang
- 10ml Sesame Oil
- 1 tsp Sesame Seeds

Pickled Cucumbers

- Sliced Cucumber
- Grated Carrots
- 20g Light Soy Sauce
- 10g Rice Vinegar
- 1 tsp Garlic Powder
- 1 tsp Chilli Flakes
- 1 tsp Sesame Seeds

Spicy Lemon Mayo

- 50g Light Mayo
- 25g Sriracha
- 1/2 tsp Garlic Powder
- 1/2 Lemond Juice
- 140g Cooked Rice per serving (560g total)
